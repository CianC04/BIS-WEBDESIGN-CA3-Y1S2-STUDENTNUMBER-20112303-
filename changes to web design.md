Header (Navigation bar) 

What I changed: I increased the size of the navigation to fit the full header. I also added a dropdown for services page. 

Why I Changed It: I increased the size of the navigation bar to fit the full length of the header because I wanted to ensure the navigation items are vertically centered and fully aligned with the logo. It made the layout mor balanced and removed the unnecessary blank space thus improving the aesthetic of the header. 

 I added the dropdown for the services page for the briefs “JavaScript elements.” 
 
Footer 

What I Changed: I added a footer section to each page of the website, even though it was not included in the original wireframes. 

Why I Changed It: In hindsight, forgetting the footer was an oversight I remembered that a footer is a standard on all websites, so I added it to improve usability by giving users easy access to social media links.

What I changed: An additional welcome message was added to the homepage, directly beneath the header. 

Why I changed it: The homepage looked empty at first and did not feel welcoming to people in or give them enough context. Adding this line made it feel more friendly and gave visitors a quick idea of what the business is about.
